Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information Games Crusher 97 (“we”) collects through the website gamescrusher97.com and our apps on Google Play, including Glass Marble Balls and M3 Player, and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

Glass Marble Balls (game)

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive your email address and display name.
  • Game data: balls, jar capacity, passes, event scores, leaderboard name and country, daily bonus progress, codes you redeem, and messages in your in-game Inbox.
  • Purchases: product, price and order ID of in-app purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or bank details.
  • Ads: the app shows ads from Google AdMob, Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network. These partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.
  • Support: feedback you send from the app.

M3 Player (music app)

  • The app plays audio files stored on your device. Your music files stay on your device and are not uploaded to us.

Website

  • Comments: your name, comment and email address. The email is stored privately and is never shown publicly (only a masked version like j*****m).
  • Feedback form: your email and message.
  • Ratings: your star rating, linked to an anonymous ID.
  • Browser storage: a random anonymous ID and small settings are saved in your browser’s local storage.
  • Free tools (currency, weather, calculators, etc.) send only the values needed to public data services; images you compress are processed in your browser and not uploaded.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress across devices.
  • To deliver purchases, rewards and redeem codes.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse.
  • To reply to feedback and support requests.
  • To show ads that keep the apps free.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, hosting)
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

4. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep game data while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and all its data at any time: email gamescrusher97@gmail.com from the email you play with. We delete it within 30 days. Order records needed by law may be kept longer.

5. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child gave us data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers and protected by access rules. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact
