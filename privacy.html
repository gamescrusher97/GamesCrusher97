Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy is for the website gamescrusher97.com (“Games Crusher 97”, “we”). It explains who owns the content on this website and what information the website collects. Our apps on Google Play each have their own privacy policy on their Play Store page.

1. Our content and ownership

  • Anyone is welcome to visit the website, use the free tools and download our apps and games for personal use.
  • You may not copy, sell, re-upload, modify, claim as your own, or otherwise misuse our content, apps, games, name or logo without our written permission. Games Crusher 97™ is our trademark.

2. Information the website collects

  • Comments: your name, comment and email address. The email is stored privately and is never shown publicly (only a masked version like j*****m).
  • Feedback form: your email and message.
  • Ratings: your star rating, linked to an anonymous ID.
  • Visits and download clicks: we count how many people open the site each day, from which country (looked up through Cloudflare from your connection; your IP address is not saved), and how many times each download button is pressed. This does not tell us who you are.
  • Browser storage: a random anonymous ID and small settings are saved in your browser’s local storage.
  • Location (optional): only if you press “use my location” in the weather tool, your browser asks for permission and the coordinates are sent to the weather service. We do not save them.
  • Camera (optional): the QR scanner uses your camera only while it is open; the picture stays on your device.
  • Free tools: currency, gold, weather and similar tools send only the values needed to public data services. Calculators and the image compressor work inside your browser; images are not uploaded.
  • Partners: if you contact us about becoming a partner, we use your message only to reply to you.

3. Ads

The website shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

4. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (database for comments, ratings, feedback and counters)
  • Google AdSense (ads) and Google Fonts
  • TradingView (stock market widget) and the donation services PayPal, Ko-fi and UPI — these are run by those companies and follow their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

To remove your comment, feedback or email from our website, email gamescrusher97@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child gave us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers and protected by access rules. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
