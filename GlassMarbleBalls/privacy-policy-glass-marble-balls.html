Privacy Policy for Glass Marble Balls

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glass Marble Balls ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. Glass Marble Balls is developed by Sahil Sheemar.

1. Information We Collect

Glass Marble Balls is a marble-guessing and aiming game. To save your progress and let you play across devices, you can create an account either by manually entering your name, email address, and country, or by signing in with your Google account. Once you have an account, we collect and store the following, tied to your email address:

  • Your name, email address, and country, as entered manually or provided by Google Sign-In.
  • Your in-game progress: ball/coin balances, jar capacity level, win/loss and game-play statistics, unlocked achievements, and Aim the Queen mode progress.
  • Your Monthly Pass subscription status (active/inactive, start and expiry dates), if you choose to subscribe.
  • Your app settings (such as music and sound toggles).

This information is stored securely using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) so your progress can be restored if you sign in again on a new device or after reinstalling the app.

2. Leaderboard

If you have an account, your name, country, and game score/stats may appear on the in-game leaderboard, visible to other players of the App. You can stop appearing on the leaderboard by not creating an account, or by contacting us to delete your account data (see "Data Deletion" below).

3. Advertising

Glass Marble Balls shows optional rewarded video ads (which you choose to watch in exchange for in-game balls/coins) using Google AdMob, with Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network as secondary/backup ad networks when AdMob has no ad available. These services may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to show and measure ads, and may use this information in line with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

4. Purchases

Glass Marble Balls offers optional real-money purchases: one-time Jar Capacity upgrades, and an auto-renewing Monthly Pass subscription. All payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details (such as card numbers) — Google handles the transaction and only tells our app whether the purchase was successful, so it can unlock the purchased content on your account. For information on how Google Play handles your payment information, see Google's own privacy policy linked above.

In-game balls/coins used for gameplay are never sold for real money and cannot be purchased directly — they are earned only through watching ads, daily bonuses, or signing up. Real-money purchases in this App only ever unlock jar capacity and Monthly Pass benefits, never additional balls/coins or any cash prize.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data described above is only used to provide the App's account, cloud-save, leaderboard, and advertising features as described in this policy, and is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network) strictly to operate those features.

6. Data Deletion

You can stop using your account at any time. To request deletion of your cloud-stored account data (profile, progress, leaderboard entry, and subscription records), please contact us using the email below. We will delete your data within a reasonable time after verifying your request.

7. Children's Privacy

Glass Marble Balls is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
