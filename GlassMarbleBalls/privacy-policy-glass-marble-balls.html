Privacy Policy for Glass Marble Balls

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glass Marble Balls ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. Glass Marble Balls is developed by Sahil Sheemar.

1. Information We Collect

Glass Marble Balls is a marble-guessing and aiming game. To save your progress and let you play across devices, you can create an account either by manually entering your name, email address, and country, or by signing in with your Google account. Once you have an account, we collect and store the following, tied to your email address:

  • Your name, email address, and country, as entered manually or provided by Google Sign-In.
  • Your in-game progress: ball/coin balances, jar capacity level, win/loss and game-play statistics, unlocked achievements, Aim the Queen mode progress, and Events progress.
  • Your Monthly Pass subscription status (active/inactive, start and expiry dates), if you choose to subscribe.
  • Your Event progress (event points, claimed event rewards, event badges, how many times you played, your daily event tickets and your best single-game score in each Event), and your Weekly Event Pass status (which Event it was bought for, start and expiry dates), if you take part in Events or buy a pass. If you redeem a voucher code, we also store the dates of the free Weekly Event Pass period it gives.
  • Your purchase records (which pack or pass was bought and a purchase token supplied by Google Play, used only to make sure each purchase is credited once).
  • Your app settings (such as music and sound toggles).
  • Messages in your in-game Inbox (for example when the Glass Marble Balls Team changed something on your account), the voucher codes you redeemed, and any warning or suspension on your account.
  • A copy of each purchase (product, price, Google Play Order ID and date), so we can find a purchase if you contact us about it.
  • Feedback you send from the in-game Feedback form (your text, and your name and e-mail if you are signed in).
  • A random ID for your phone (not your advertising ID) with the time you last opened the App, the time you last played a game, whether you are signed in, and the App version. We use it only to count how many players are online or active.

This information is stored securely using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Firebase Authentication) so your progress can be restored if you sign in again on a new device or after reinstalling the app.

When your phone has no internet, the App keeps a separate copy of your balls and progress on the phone only, so you can still play. This offline copy is never mixed with your online account and is erased when the internet is back; your online account is then loaded again. You cannot make purchases while offline.

1A. Account Safety and Admin Review

To keep the game fair and safe, the Glass Marble Balls Team (Games Crusher 97) can view player accounts and their game data through a private admin page that only our own account can open. We use it to fix problems (for example to give balls, jar capacity or a pass that a player paid for but did not receive), to answer feedback, and to check for cheating, hacking or misuse of the App. If we find misuse, we may correct the account (for example remove balls or a pass that were not earned or paid for), send a warning, or suspend the account for a time or permanently. Every change we make to your account is shown to you as a message in your in-game Inbox (Settings).

1B. Online Event Games

The weekly Event mini-games are loaded from our website (gamescrusher97.com) when you open Events, so they can be updated without an app update. An internet connection is needed for Events. The game page receives no personal information; your points, tickets and rewards are handled by the App and your account.

2. Leaderboard

If you have an account, your name, country, and game score/stats may appear on the in-game leaderboard, visible to other players of the App. Each Event also has its own Event leaderboard that shows the name, country and best single-game score of players who are signed in with Google; it is public to other players and starts fresh with every Event (older Event boards are deleted after a while). You can stop appearing on the leaderboard by not creating an account, or by contacting us to delete your account data (see "Data Deletion" below).

3. Advertising

Glass Marble Balls shows optional rewarded video ads (which you choose to watch in exchange for in-game balls/coins) using Google AdMob, with Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network as secondary/backup ad networks when AdMob has no ad available. These services may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to show and measure ads, and may use this information in line with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

4. Purchases

Glass Marble Balls offers optional real-money purchases: one-time Jar Capacity upgrades, one-time packs of balls (50 balls for 5 AED, 100 balls for 10 AED, 250 balls for 25 AED), an auto-renewing Monthly Pass subscription, and a one-time Weekly Event Pass (valid for one festival Event only). All payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details (such as card numbers) — Google handles the transaction and only tells our app whether the purchase was successful, so it can unlock the purchased content on your account. For information on how Google Play handles your payment information, see Google's own privacy policy linked above.

Balls are virtual in-game items used only to play inside the App. They can be earned (ads, daily bonus, signing up, Events) or bought in packs, but they have no cash value, cannot be sold, exchanged for money or sent to another player, and the App offers no real-money prize or payout. A pack of balls is credited only if it fits in your jar; if it does not fit, you are asked to make room first and you are charged only when the balls fit. Real-money purchases otherwise only unlock jar capacity, Monthly Pass benefits and Weekly Event Pass benefits (such as 2X event points, more daily event tickets and a badge). Event rewards are free in-game balls and badges with no cash value.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data described above is only used to provide the App's account, cloud-save, leaderboard, Inbox, support, safety and advertising features as described in this policy, and is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network) strictly to operate those features.

6. Data Deletion

You can stop using your account at any time. To request deletion of your cloud-stored account data (profile, progress, event progress, Event leaderboard entries, and purchase, subscription and pass records), please contact us using the email below. We will delete your data within a reasonable time after verifying your request.

7. Children's Privacy

Glass Marble Balls is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
