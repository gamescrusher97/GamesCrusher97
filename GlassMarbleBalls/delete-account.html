Delete your Glass Marble Balls account

App: Glass Marble Balls (developer: Sahil Sheemar) · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Glass Marble Balls and make sure you are signed in with Google.
  2. Tap your Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom, then Yes, delete everything.
  4. Choose the same Google account to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: without the app

Send an email to gamescrusher97@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Glass Marble Balls account", sent from the Google account (email address) you used in the game. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email address, country) and your sign-in account.
  • Your balls, jar capacity, game statistics, achievements, Event progress and Event leaderboard entries.
  • Your Monthly Pass and Weekly Event Pass status, redeemed voucher codes and your in-game Inbox.
  • Your purchase, subscription and pass records stored by us.

What is kept

  • Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases (we cannot delete those; see Google's privacy policy).
  • Purchases already made are not refunded when an account is deleted. To cancel the Monthly Pass, cancel it in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions).
