1. The App

Glass Marble Balls is a marble-guessing and aiming game ("Guess the Balls" and "Aim the Queen" modes, plus limited-time festival Events with their own mini-games), free to download and play. You can create an account by signing in manually (name, email, country) or with HUAWEI ID sign-in to save progress, appear on the leaderboard, and sync across devices.

2. In-Game Balls — No Real-Money Prizes

Balls are virtual items used only to play inside the App. They can be earned (optional ads, the daily bonus, signing up, Events) or bought in packs through HUAWEI AppGallery (see section 3A). Balls have no cash value: they cannot be sold, exchanged for money, or transferred or sent to another player, and the App offers no real-money prize, payout, or cash value for any in-game win. Buying balls does not buy a chance to win money.

3. Jar Capacity Purchases

The App offers optional one-time real-money purchases that increase your jar's ball-holding capacity ("Buy Capacity" tiers). These are one-time purchases processed by HUAWEI In-App Purchases (AppGallery) — you pay once and the capacity increase stays on the HUAWEI ID that made the purchase, with no recurring charge.

3A. Buy Balls

  • Balls can be bought as one-time packs through HUAWEI In-App Purchases (AppGallery): 50 balls for 5 AED, 100 balls for 10 AED, 250 balls for 25 AED.
  • A pack can only be bought if the whole pack fits in your jar. If it does not fit, the App tells you to remove some balls or buy more jar capacity first, and you are charged only when the balls fit.
  • You must be online and signed in with HUAWEI ID to buy, so the balls are saved on our server. The purchase is checked with Huawei before the balls are credited, and each purchase is credited once.
  • Bought balls are virtual, have no cash value and cannot be sold or sent to anyone.

4. Monthly Pass Subscription

  • Monthly Pass is 25 AED, billed every 30 days, auto-renewing through AppGallery until cancelled.
  • All Monthly Pass benefits (No Ads, extra daily reward, diamond badge, +500 Cloud Space jar capacity, exclusive look) apply only while the pass is currently active.
  • If the pass expires or is cancelled, benefits are removed starting from your next game round — any round or Aim the Queen session already in progress when it lapses keeps its benefits until that round/session ends.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not refund the current billing period, which the Monthly Pass stays active for.
  • Jar capacity gained from Monthly Pass ("Cloud Space") is separate from capacity bought outright or earned via ads/daily bonus — only the Monthly Pass portion is removed if the pass lapses.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription anytime from within the App, or directly from your AppGallery subscriptions page.
  • A Monthly Pass given by a voucher code (not bought) also gives a free Weekly Event Pass for each Event that is running when the code is redeemed or that starts within the 30 days of that pass; each such free pass lasts until its Event ends. A Monthly Pass bought through AppGallery does not include this.

5. Events and Weekly Event Pass

  • The App runs limited-time festival Events (for example Eid, Diwali, Holi, Halloween, Christmas, New Year and other major festivals). Each Event lasts about one week, starts and ends automatically on the dates built into the App, and has its own mini-game, event points and reward ladder. When an Event ends, its mini-game, points and unclaimed rewards are removed; the next Event has its own mini-game. An Event may be shortened, changed or cancelled because of a technical problem.
  • Event rewards are free in-game balls and cosmetic badges only. They have no cash value, are never sold for real money, and no real-money prize or payout is offered. Rewards follow your jar capacity and, where the Monthly Pass is active, its 2X reward benefit.
  • Weekly Event Pass: an optional one-time purchase of 15 AED through HUAWEI In-App Purchases (AppGallery), valid only for the single Event it is bought for and ending when that Event ends (about one week). It does not renew automatically and nothing carries over to the next Event.
  • Each game in an Event uses 1 ticket. You get 5 tickets a day; while a Weekly Event Pass is active you get 10 a day. 10 balls can be turned into 1 ticket, never above that day's number of tickets.
  • While active, the Weekly Event Pass gives 2X event points, 10 tickets a day instead of 5, and that Event's exclusive badge, which stays in your collection afterwards. It does not give balls directly and has no cash value.
  • Every Event has its own leaderboard of each signed-in player's best single-game score, shown publicly with name and country, and reset with every Event.
  • When no Event is running, the Events button is locked and only shows the time left until the next Event.
  • The Weekly Event Pass is completely separate from the Monthly Pass: buying, holding, or losing one does not change the other.
  • You must be signed in with HUAWEI ID to buy a Weekly Event Pass, so that it is saved on our server. The purchase is checked with Huawei before it is credited.
