They work together with our Business page (prices) and our Business Privacy Policy. By paying or confirming an order on WhatsApp or email, you agree to these terms. If a written quote we send you says something different, the quote wins for that order.

1. What we make

  • Business website: up to 5 pages, mobile-friendly, with your services, prices, photos, location, and WhatsApp and call buttons, set up for Google search.
  • Apps and games: made to a written quote that lists what the app will do, the price and the time.
  • Anything not listed in the order or the quote is extra work, and we give you a price first.

2. Free sample

Before you pay, we can make a free sample of your home page. If you do not want to go ahead, you pay nothing and we delete the sample. The sample stays ours until you order.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices are the ones on our Business page on the day you order, or in your written quote.
  • Setup fee: 50% when you approve the sample and we start the full work, and 50% before the website goes live.
  • Monthly plan: paid in advance at the start of each month, from the day the website goes live.
  • If a tax such as VAT (UAE) or GST (India) applies, we tell you before you pay.
  • We send a payment receipt for every payment.

4. Late payment

  • If a monthly payment is more than 15 days late, we may pause the website until it is paid.
  • If it is more than 60 days late, the plan ends. We keep your files for 30 more days so you can pay and restart, or ask for them under section 8.

5. What the monthly plan includes

  • Hosting, a secure https lock, checks and backups.
  • A .com domain free while the plan is active. A .ae domain (AED 199 a year) or .in domain (₹1,499 a year) is paid extra, every year, in advance.
  • Up to 3 small changes a month, such as new prices, photos, timings, phone number or offers. Unused changes do not carry over to the next month.
  • New pages, new designs or new features are extra and priced first.
  • We usually make small changes within 2 working days.

6. Your domain name

  • We register the domain in your business name, and we manage it for you while the plan is active.
  • If you stop the plan, we move the domain to your own account at a domain company of your choice. If the free .com domain is less than 12 months old at that time, a domain fee of AED 45 or ₹1,000 applies.
  • We are not responsible if a domain you asked for is already taken, or for rules set by the domain registry.

  • After the setup fee is paid in full, you own your website's design and content.

10. Times

A normal business website is usually ready within about 7 days after we get all your content. Times can change if content arrives late or the work grows. We keep you updated on WhatsApp.

  • We may end a plan with 30 days' notice. If we do, we refund any unused part you paid in advance and hand over your files and domain.
