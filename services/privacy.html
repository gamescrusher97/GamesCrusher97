Business Privacy Policy

Website, app and maintenance services · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Games Crusher 97 uses the details of businesses we contact and of clients who order a website, app, game or maintenance plan. For visitors to our own website and free tools, see our main Privacy Policy.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, business name, phone or WhatsApp number, and email.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, email or phone about your order.
  • Website content: the text, photos, logo, prices, address and timings you give us for your website or app.
  • Domain details: the owner name and contact details that domain companies require to register a domain.
  • Payment records: what you paid, when and how. We do not keep your card or bank login details.

2. Public business details

To find businesses that may need a website, we look at public business listings such as Google Maps (business name, public phone number, rating and address). We contact a business at most once with a short message. If you reply STOP or tell us you are not interested, we do not contact you again and we remove your details from our list.

3. Why we use your details

  • To make, run and update your website, app or game.
  • To register and manage your domain in your business name.
  • To talk to you about your order, send receipts and remind you about payments.
  • To keep the records the law asks a business to keep.

We do not sell your details, and we do not send you advertising from other companies.

4. Who we share them with

Only with the services needed to do the work, and only what they need:

  • Domain companies (for example Cloudflare or Porkbun), to register your domain. Where possible, privacy protection is turned on so your personal details are hidden from public lookups.
  • Hosting services (for example GitHub Pages, Cloudflare or Google Firebase), which keep your website online.
  • App stores (Google Play, Apple), when an app is published on your account.
  • WhatsApp and email providers, which carry our messages.
  • Government or courts, only if the law requires it.

5. Your customers' details

If your website has a contact or booking form, the messages your customers send belong to you. We only set up and run the form for you; we do not use those details for anything else. You are responsible for telling your customers how you use their details, and we can add a simple privacy page to your website for this.

6. How long we keep them

  • For as long as you are our client, and then for up to 5 years for payment and tax records, as business law requires.
  • Website content and files: until 30 days after the plan ends and your files are handed over, then deleted.
  • Businesses we contacted but who did not become clients: deleted within 6 months, or at once if you ask.

7. Keeping them safe

Accounts for hosting, domains and email are protected with strong passwords and two-step login. Only we can see your details.

8. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details (except records we must keep by law), or to stop contacting you. Just send a message on WhatsApp or email.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
