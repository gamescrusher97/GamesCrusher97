Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. All PDF work happens inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I convert JPG to PDF on my phone?

Yes. Choose Photos to PDF, pick one or more photos, and download the PDF.

Why is the compressed PDF text not selectable?

Compress PDF turns each page into a picture to make it smaller, so the text becomes part of the image.
