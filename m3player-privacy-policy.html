Privacy Policy for M3 Player

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how M3 Player ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. M3 Player is developed by Sahil Sheemar.

1. Information We Collect

M3 Player is primarily an offline music player that works with audio files already stored on your device. Most of the app's features (playing, browsing, favoriting, creating playlists, using the equalizer, and cutting or merging audio locally) work entirely on your device and do not send any data anywhere.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we additionally collect and store the following, tied to your Google account email address:

  • Your name and email address, as provided by Google Sign-In, to identify your account.
  • Your list of favorite songs (song title and artist only - not the audio files themselves).
  • Your playlists (playlist names and which songs are in them, referenced by ID - not the audio files themselves).
  • Whether the Smart Equalizer is turned on or off.
  • Your app preferences: chosen theme (Normal or Dark mode) and your Default Save Location setting (App folder or Downloads folder).

This information is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication) so that it can be restored if you sign in again on a new device or after reinstalling the app.

2. Audio Files

M3 Player reads the music files already present on your device's storage in order to list and play them, and to create cut or merged audio files using the Edit Music tool. Your audio files are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party - all cutting and merging happens locally on your device, and the resulting file is saved to the location you choose in Settings (App folder, Downloads, or a custom folder you select).

3. Advertising

M3 Player shows ads using Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as a secondary ad network. These services may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to show and measure ads, and may use this information in line with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Permissions

M3 Player requests access to your device's audio/media files (storage permission) solely to find, play, and edit the songs already on your device. This access is not used to read or share any other type of file.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data described above is only used to provide the app's cloud-sync and advertising features as described in this policy, and is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Unity Ads) strictly to operate those features.

6. Data Deletion

You can sign out of your Google account inside the app's Settings at any time, which stops further syncing. To request deletion of your cloud-stored data (favorites, playlists, and settings tied to your account), please contact us using the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

M3 Player is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
