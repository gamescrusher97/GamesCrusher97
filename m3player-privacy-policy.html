Privacy Policy for M3 Player

Last updated: September 2026

M3 Player ("we", "our", or "the app") is a music player application developed by Sahil Sheemar. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

M3 Player does not require account creation to use its core features. If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your email address to identify your account.

If you submit feedback through the app, the feedback message text you provide is collected and stored.

How We Use Information

  • Email address: used only for account identification if you choose to sign in.
  • Feedback messages: used to improve the app and respond to user suggestions or issues.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by service providers (Google Firebase) that host our sign-in and feedback storage infrastructure.

Data Security

All data is transmitted securely using encryption (HTTPS) and stored using Google Firebase services.

Advertising

M3 Player displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's own privacy policy, to show relevant ads.

Data Deletion

If you would like your account data or feedback deleted, please contact us using the email below.
